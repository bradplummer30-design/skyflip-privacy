Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SKYFLIP ("we," "our," or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile game, available on iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

SKYFLIP does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address. We do not require account creation or login to play.

The App stores game progress, preferences, and settings (such as coins, unlocked skins, and high scores) locally on your device. SKYFLIP does not upload your local save data to our own servers, and we do not operate servers, databases, or cloud storage for user data. Other sections of this policy describe information handled by our partners: scores and achievement progress submitted to Apple Game Center when you are signed in (Section 5), advertising-related data handled by Unity Ads (Sections 2 and 3), and in-app purchases processed by Apple (Section 4).

2. Third-Party Advertising

We use Unity Ads to display advertisements in the App, including rewarded video ads, interstitial ads, and banner ads. Unity Ads may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location (country/region level)
  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completion)

This data is collected and processed by Unity Technologies in accordance with their privacy policy. You can review Unity's privacy policy at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS devices, we request your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before initializing Unity Ads. This permission controls whether apps may access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). You may choose to allow or deny this request. Declining does not prevent you from playing: if you deny tracking, you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to your interests. You can change your tracking preference at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4. In-App Purchases

SKYFLIP offers optional in-app purchases, including coin packs and an option to remove ads. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, process, or store any payment information, credit card details, or billing data.

SKYFLIP keeps a local record of limited purchase-state information, including the Remove Ads entitlement and a short list of transaction identifiers used to prevent duplicate grants. The locally stored record is not payment-card or billing information. You can restore the Remove Ads purchase on a new device with the Restore Purchases button.

5. Game Center

The App integrates with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. When you are signed in to Game Center, SKYFLIP submits your scores to Game Center leaderboards and reports your achievement progress and completion to Game Center. Game Center is an Apple service, and your use of it is subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices. SKYFLIP does not receive or store your Apple ID credentials or payment details; sign-in and identity are handled by Apple.

6. Data Storage

Your game data (progress, settings, currencies, and unlocks) is stored locally on your device. SKYFLIP does not upload your local save file to our own servers, and we do not operate servers, databases, or cloud storage for user data. When you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores and achievement progress are submitted to Game Center (Section 5); advertising-related data is handled by Unity Ads (Sections 2 and 3). If you uninstall the App, your local game data will be deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

SKYFLIP is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain rights under GDPR. Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, data subject requests should be directed to our third-party partners (Unity Ads or Apple). You have the right to opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings and withdraw consent for ad tracking at any time.

9. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, the CCPA provides you with specific rights. We do not sell personal information. To opt out of personalized advertising, adjust your device's ad tracking settings or deny the ATT prompt on iOS.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
